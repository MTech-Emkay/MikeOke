About Us

Who We Are

MikeOke Motors is an automobile company that was registered under the CAC in 2017 but has been in the automobile business before then. We have worked in honesty and transparency with our customers and has gained so much experience in the automobile business. It was started with the core objective of making sure customers never have to stress before buying, driving and enjoying their next dream car. With years of experience, we have been able to help customers make the best and confident decision of vehicles to purchase.

Our Mission

To make buying, selling, and importing vehicles simple, reliable, and accessible by providing quality vehicles, budget-friendly solutions, and customer-focused services built on integrity and trust.

Our Vission

To become a trusted and leading name in Nigeria's automotive industry, recognized for excellence, transparency, and innovative solutions that make vehicle ownership easier for people.
