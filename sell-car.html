SELL YOUR CAR WITH MIKEOKE MOTORS

Looking To Sell Your Car?

Looking to sell your car without the stress of finding the right buyer yourself? MikeOke Motors is here to help you take the next step with confidence. Get in touch with us to discuss your vehicle and explore the way forward.

How It Works

Selling Options

Sell Your Car Directly To Us

If your vehicle meets our purchasing requrements, we can asess it and discuss a possible direct purchase by the company

We Help You Find A Buyer

If your vehicle does not meets our purchasing requrements or you prefer to sell to another buyer, we can asess it and help you find a suitable buyer

How It Works

Sell Your Car In A Few Simple Steps

  1. Contact Us

    Contact MikeOke Motors by whatsapp, phone or email and let us know that you want to sell your car

  2. Share Vehicle Details

    Provide the details about your car like the make, model, year, condition, location, asking price(if you have one) and photographs.

  3. Discuss Selling Options

    We'll discuss whether a direct purchase may be suitable or whether we can assist you in finding a buyer.

  4. Discuss Inspection And Pricing

    We'll discuss the vehicle with you and explain any inspection and evaluation steps needed before an offer can be agreed.

  5. Agree On Next Steps

    After agreement to inspection terms, pricing, payment arrangements, and necessary documentations.Next steps can be discussed and completed.

Tell us about your car

Informations To Prepare

  • Vehicle Details

    Make, model, year, transmission, mileage, and fuel type.

  • Vehicle Condition

    General condition, known faults, maintenance history, and any relevant accident or repair history.

  • Price Expectations

    Your asking price or your preferred selling range.

  • Vehicle Location

    The city or area where the car is currently located.

  • Photographs

    Clear photographs of the exterior, interior, dashboard, and any notable damage, if present.

  • Vehicle Documentation

    Photocopy or Original copy of vehicle's ownership documents and other necessary documents

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What types of cars can I sell through MikeOke Motors?

    Contact us with your vehicle's make, model, year, condition, and location. We'll discuss whether we can assist with your particular vehicle.

  • Will MikeOke Motors buy my car directly?

    We may consider your vehicle for direct purchase depending on its make, model, condition and our overall purchasing requrements. Otherwise, we just help you find a suitable buyer

  • How is the selling price determined?

    You can communicate your asking price and expectations.However the price can depend on factors such as the vehicle's make, model, year, mileage, condition, service history, and current market demand. An appropriate assessment may be needed before an offer is agreed.
