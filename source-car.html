How Our Car Sourcing Process Works

  1. Tell Us What You Need

    Contact us with your preferred car, specifications, intended use, and budget.

  2. We Explore Suitable Options

    We work to identify vehicles that match your requirements and discuss the options we can source.

  3. Review Your Options and Budget

    We discuss vehicle pricing and relevant additional costs so you can make an informed decision.

  4. Confirm the Next Steps

    Once you choose an option, we discuss availability, payment arrangements, and the next steps for acquiring your vehicle.

Help Us Find Your Preferred Car

When contacting MikeOke Motors, try to provide:

  • Preferred car make and model
  • Preferred year or year range
  • Vehicle condition: Nigeria-used or foreign-used
  • Your budget
  • Preferred colour and features
  • Your location
  • Any special requirements

You don't need to know every detail before contacting us. We can discuss your preferences together.
