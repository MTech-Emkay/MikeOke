UNDERSTAND THE COST BEFORE YOU IMPORT

Budget And Important Considerations

The cost of importing a car goes beyond its purchase price.Planning ahead helps you understand the financial commitment involved.

  • Vehicle purchase price

    The actual amount of the selected car, influenced by its model, year, condition, specifications, etc.

  • Shipping And Handling

    Transport, shipping, and any applicable port or handling charges

  • Duties, Taxes and Clearing

    Applicable import charges and clearing costs

  • Other Relevant Expenses

    Depending on the arrangements, these may include inspection, delivery, etc.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can I import a specific car of my choice?

    Yes, you can tell us your preferred make, model, year, trim, and other specifications. We'll discuss sourcing options and availability

  • How much does it cost to import a car to Nigeria?

    The total depends on the vehicle price, shipping, applicable duties and taxes, clearing, and other expenses.

  • How long does car importation takes?

    The timeframe depends on vehicle location, sourcing, seller readiness, shipping schedules, customs procedures, and delivery arrangements. We cannot guarantee a fixed timeline without details of the shipment.
