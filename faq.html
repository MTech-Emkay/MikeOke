Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about buying, selling, sourcing, and importing vehicles with MikeOke Motors.

Buying A Car

  • How can I buy a car from MikeOke Motors?

    Browse our available vehicles, choose the car that suits your needs and budget, and contact our team for more information. We'll guide you through the purchase process, payment arrangements, and delivery options.

  • Are your vehicles inspected before sale?

    We encourage customers to confirm the vehicle's condition and inspection details before purchase. Contact our team to discuss the inspection information available for your preferred vehicle.

  • Can I request more pictures or videos of a vehicle?

    Yes. Contact us with the vehicle you're interested in, and we'll help you obtain additional pictures or videos where available.

  • Are the prices of your vehicles negotiable?

    Vehicle prices may vary depending on the car, condition, and market value. Contact us to discuss the listed price and any available negotiation options.

  • Do you offer delivery services?

    We offer assistance with vehicle delivery arrangements. Contact us to confirm delivery availability, the destination, and any applicable charges.

Selling A Car

  • How can I sell my car through MikeOke Motors?

    Contact us with your vehicle details, including the make, model, year, condition, mileage, and location. Our team will review the information and explain the available selling options.

  • What types of cars can I sell through MikeOke Motors?

    Contact us with your vehicle's make, model, year, condition, and location. We'll discuss whether we can assist with your particular vehicle.

  • Will MikeOke Motors buy my car directly?

    We may consider your vehicle for direct purchase depending on its make, model, condition and our overall purchasing requrements. Otherwise, we just help you find a suitable buyer

  • How is the selling price determined?

    You can communicate your asking price and expectations.However the price can depend on factors such as the vehicle's make, model, year, mileage, condition, service history, and current market demand. An appropriate assessment may be needed before an offer is agreed.

  • Do I need to visit your office to get started?

    You can begin by contacting us remotely through an available contact channel. We will explain any further steps, including whether a physical inspection is needed

  • What documents will I need?

    Ownership and vehicle documents may be required to verify the vehicle and complete the sale. Contact us for guidance on the documents relevant to your circumstances

  • How long does it take to sell my car?

    The timeframe depends on the vehicle, its condition, the agreed price, inspection requirements, and the arrangements between both parties. Contact us to discuss your particular situation.

  • Do I pay a fee for buyer-finding assistance?

    Yes.Any applicable fees or commission would be explained and agreed upon before the service begins.

Car Sourcing

  • Can you source a car brand that is not listed on your website?

    Yes. Tell us the vehicle you want, and we will explore suitable sourcing options based on your specifications and budget.

  • Can I request a specific make, model, or year?

    Yes. You can provide your preferred make, model, year, and other important features. Availability will depend on the options we can find.

  • Can you source both Nigeria-used and foreign-used cars?

    Yes. You can tell us which condition you prefer, and we will discuss suitable available options.

  • Can you source a car within my budget?

    We help you plan your budget and explore vehicles that may suit it. The final options depend on vehicle availability, condition, and pricing.

  • Can you source a car from abroad?

    Yes. If your preferred vehicle needs to be imported, we can discuss the process through our Import Your Car service.

  • How do I get started?

    Contact us through WhatsApp, phone, or email with your preferred vehicle details and budget. We will discuss your requirements and the next steps.

Importing a Car

  • Can I import a specific car of my choice?

    Yes, you can tell us your preferred make, model, year, trim, and other specifications. We'll discuss sourcing options and availability

  • How much does it cost to import a car to Nigeria?

    The total depends on the vehicle price, shipping, applicable duties and taxes, clearing, and other expenses.

  • How long does car importation takes?

    The timeframe depends on vehicle location, sourcing, seller readiness, shipping schedules, customs procedures, and delivery arrangements. We cannot guarantee a fixed timeline without details of the shipment.

  • Can you help with both shipping and clearing?

    Yes, we can help you with the entire importation process.

  • Can I import a car within a specific budget?

    You can share your budget and preferred specifications. We'll discuss suitable options, taking the estimated total import cost into account.

  • What documents are required to import a car?

    Requirements depend on the vehicle and the applicable import procedures. We'll guide you on the relevant documentation and where professional clearing assistance may be needed.

Payment, Delivery and Documentation

  • What payment methods do you accept?

    Please contact our team to confirm the currently accepted payment methods and the appropriate payment procedure for your transaction.

  • How do I receive my vehicle after purchase?

    Once the transaction and necessary documentation are completed, we'll discuss the available collection or delivery arrangements with you.

  • What documents come with a purchased vehicle?

    The documents provided depend on the vehicle's ownership history, registration status, and transaction. We'll clarify which documents are available and applicable before the purchase is completed.

  • Can I arrange vehicle delivery to another location?

    You can contact us to discuss delivery to your preferred destination. Availability, delivery timelines, and applicable charges will be confirmed based on the location.

General Questions

  • Where is MikeOke Motors located?

    You can check the Contact Us page on our website for available location information.

  • How can I contact your team?

    You can reach us through the phone, WhatsApp, or email links provided on our website.Go to our contacts page for more info. We're happy to discuss your vehicle needs and answer your questions.

  • Do you operate outside your immediate location?

    We can discuss vehicle sourcing, importation, and delivery arrangements based on your location and requirements. Contact us to confirm the services available in your area.

  • What are your business hours?

    Our current business hours will be displayed on our Contact Us page. We recommend contacting us during those hours for assistance.

Still Have Questions?
